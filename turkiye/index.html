🧭 How this fits the classic 3-area split — and the golden hours

Guides split Istanbul into three areas, and this trip does too — compressed into the days you actually have. Sun = Sultanahmet / Old Town (the historic peninsula: Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, Cistern, Süleymaniye). Mon = Galata / Beyoğlu + the bazaars (Topkapı first, then Grand & Spice Bazaars, Galata Tower at golden hour). The Asian side (Kadıköy / Üsküdar) is Fri evening — the only window left after Cappadocia. You land at 18:20 and sunset is 17:39, so it's an evening/dinner play, not a sunset one: it lives as swap-ready alternatives on Fri's dinner slot (tap Develi → Alternatives) instead of breaking the golden path.

🌅🌇 Golden hours: Istanbul sunrise ~08:00 / sunset ~17:40 (golden ~08:00–08:50 & ~16:55–17:40) · Göreme sunrise ~07:35 / sunset ~17:15 (golden ~07:35–08:20 & ~16:30–17:15). Stops placed inside a golden window carry a 🌅/🌇 chip — Blue Mosque at 08:30 opening, the 17:00 Bosphorus sailing, Galata Tower at 16:30, terrace balloons at dawn, Uçhisar + the sunset point at dusk. Every day card shows its own sunrise/sunset line, its own map sits beside the plan (tap any orange line for transport, any pin for its time window), and every big sight carries a ⏱ crowd line — best slot + expected wait.
